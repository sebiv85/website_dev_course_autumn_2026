Another way of seeing the world

I'm not a travel journalist, just someone who enjoys discovering places and seeing what's around the next corner.

The destinations you'll find here aren't necessarily the usual tourist hotspots. They're simply places I've visited, moments I've captured and little details that made me stop and look.

The world looks very different when you go a little off the beaten path. Sometimes the most interesting things are the ones you stumble upon by accident.

I hope you enjoy exploring them through my photographs – and perhaps you'll find a place that makes you want to pack your bags and see it for yourself.
